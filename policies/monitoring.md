# Cardiac Monitoring and Level of Care Policy

Policy BM-POL-004 · Version 2 · Effective 2025-11-10 · Riverbend General Hospital (fictional)

## 1. Levels of care

Riverbend General Hospital has four inpatient levels of care. Medical wards take general adult patients. Telemetry wards take patients who need continuous cardiac monitoring but not intensive care. The intensive care unit takes patients who need organ support or one-to-one nursing. Surgical wards take patients before and after an operation.

## 2. Monitoring requirement

Whether a patient needs cardiac monitoring is a clinical decision recorded by the admitting clinician. The ward a patient is sent to does not by itself settle the question: the need for monitoring must be stated for every placement request, including requests for a telemetry bed.

## 3. Monitored beds

A patient who needs monitoring must be placed in a monitoring-capable bed. Medical ward beds at Riverbend are not monitoring-capable, so a medical patient who needs monitoring cannot be placed on a medical ward and the request must be escalated for a decision about level of care.

## 4. Placing a patient outside the requested level of care

Staff must not place a patient in a different level of care because the requested ward is full. Placing a medical patient in a telemetry or intensive care bed, or the reverse, requires a clinical decision and is arranged through escalation.
