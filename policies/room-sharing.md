# Room Sharing Policy

Policy BM-POL-003 · Version 4 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Riverbend General Hospital (fictional)

## 1. Principle

Shared rooms are used to make full use of capacity while protecting patients' privacy and dignity. The rules in this policy are local policy for Riverbend General Hospital. Each shared room carries its own sharing rule, so that individual rooms can be configured differently.

## 2. Same-sex shared rooms

Unless a room is configured otherwise, a shared room accommodates patients of the same recorded sex. A patient may be placed in an empty shared room regardless of sex. A patient may be placed in a partly occupied shared room only when their recorded sex matches that of the patients already in the room.

## 3. When compatibility cannot be determined

If the recorded sex of the incoming patient, or of any patient already in the room, is unknown, compatibility cannot be determined. Staff must not guess. The placement requires review by the nurse in charge, and in the meantime the patient may be offered an empty shared room or a private room if one is eligible.

## 4. Private rooms

Private rooms have no sharing rule and are not subject to this policy. A private room is required when the clinician requests one, for example for end-of-life care, safeguarding concerns or severe immunosuppression. A patient's preference alone does not make a private room a requirement.

## 5. Exceptions

A shared room may be used across sexes only in an intensive care setting where clinical need overrides, and only with the approval of the nurse in charge, which must be recorded. No other exceptions are permitted.
