# Isolation Placement Policy

Policy BM-POL-002 · Version 2 · Effective 2025-09-01 · Riverbend General Hospital (fictional)

## 1. When isolation is required

Isolation is required for a patient under contact, droplet or airborne precautions, as decided by the admitting clinician or the infection prevention team. Bed management staff do not decide whether a patient needs isolation; they place the patient according to the decision recorded for them.

## 2. Where an isolation patient may be placed

A patient who needs isolation must be placed in an isolation-capable bed. Isolation-capable beds are in rooms with a door that closes, a hand-wash basin at the entrance and space for protective equipment. A standard bed must never be used for an isolation patient, including as a temporary measure.

## 3. When no isolation bed is free

If no isolation-capable bed is eligible, the request must be escalated to the bed management coordinator and the infection prevention nurse on call. Staff must not place the patient in a standard bed while waiting. The patient remains under precautions in their current location until a suitable bed is confirmed.

## 4. Cleaning after an isolation patient

A bed vacated by an isolation patient requires a terminal clean before it can be marked available. The target turnaround is 90 minutes. The bed stays in cleaning status until environmental services sign it off.
