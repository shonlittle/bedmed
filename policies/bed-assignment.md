# Bed Assignment Policy

Policy BM-POL-001 · Version 3 · Effective 2026-01-15 · Riverbend General Hospital (fictional)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how inpatient beds are matched to patients at Riverbend General Hospital. It applies to all admissions and transfers to the medical, telemetry, intensive care and surgical wards. It does not cover the emergency department, maternity or paediatrics.

## 2. Availability and eligibility

A bed that is free is not automatically suitable. A bed may be offered to a patient only when all of the following are true: the bed is in a ward of the level of care the patient needs; the ward is open to admissions; the bed's status is available; the bed has any equipment the patient needs, such as cardiac monitoring; the room meets any isolation or private-room requirement; and the room-sharing policy allows the placement.

## 3. Bed statuses

Every bed has one status. Available means cleaned and ready. Occupied means a patient is assigned. Cleaning means environmental services are preparing the bed; the target turnaround is 45 minutes for a standard clean and 90 minutes after an isolation patient. Reserved means the bed is held for a named incoming patient. Out of service means the bed cannot be used because of a fault or maintenance.

## 4. Reserved beds

A bed may be reserved for a patient who is expected within two hours, for example a patient leaving the operating theatre. A reservation lapses after two hours unless the bed management coordinator renews it. A reserved bed must not be given to another patient while the reservation stands, even if that patient is waiting.

## 5. Choosing between eligible beds

When more than one bed is eligible, staff should prefer the bed that leaves specialised capacity free. A patient who does not need isolation should not be placed in an isolation-capable room, and a patient who does not need monitoring should not take a monitored bed, when a standard bed is eligible. Private rooms are kept for patients who need them.

## 6. Confirming an assignment

An assignment is complete only when it is recorded in the bed management system. A verbal agreement or a plan to assign is not an assignment. The person requesting the bed must confirm the proposed bed before it is recorded, and must be told if the bed is no longer available at the moment of recording.
